Product Profile

The Ragdoll Cat

Large, affectionate, and strikingly beautiful. Recognized instantly by their vivid blue eyes and distinct plush point coats.

Temperament

Docile & Devoted

True to their name, Ragdolls are famous for completely relaxing and going limp when held. They exhibit puppy-like behavior, often following you from room to room.
