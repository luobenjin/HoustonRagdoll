Traditional & Modern Face: Q&A

What is the difference?

Traditional Ragdolls have longer, wedge-shaped faces, narrower muzzles, longer noses, and more oval-shaped eyes, giving them an elegant appearance. Modern-style Ragdolls have rounder faces, fuller muzzles, shorter noses, wider-set ears, and larger-looking eyes, giving them a sweeter, more kitten-like appearance even as adults.

Are they separate Ragdoll types?

No. “Traditional” and “new face” are not separate Ragdoll breeds or official categories in the CFA breed standard. CFA describes the Ragdoll head as a large, modified wedge. A cat's appearance can also change as it grows, and photos or angles can make features look different.

Does face type determine personality?

No. Face shape alone does not tell you a cat's temperament. When choosing a kitten, consider its individual personality, health records, and how it was raised, along with the look you prefer.

For the formal description, see the CFA Ragdoll breed standard.
